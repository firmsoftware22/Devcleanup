# 🧹 DevCleanup – Instant Windows Performance & Cleanup Tool

**DevCleanup** is a lightweight, fast, and safe PowerShell utility for Windows designed to clean up your system right after finishing your work or coding session. 

Have you ever wondered why your PC slows down over the day? Many applications, background services, and development runtimes (like **Node.js** or **Python**) keep running silently in the background even after you close their windows—eating up valuable RAM and CPU. With a simple double-click on your desktop, DevCleanup safely terminates unnecessary background processes and removes temporary junk files, making your PC noticeably faster in seconds.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🚀 Instant Speed Boost:** Terminates idle background applications and lingering development processes (including **Node.js** and **Python** instances). You will be surprised how much was running unnecessarily in the background!
* **🗑️ Safe Temporary Junk Cleanup:** Cleans up temporary system and cache files that slow down your machine—without touching any critical Windows files.
* **🛡️ 100% Data & Privacy Safe:** Your personal files, documents, your **Downloads folder**, and even your **Recycle Bin** remain completely untouched.
* **🔄 Non-Destructive & Reversible:** Stopped programs are not uninstalled or broken—they will simply start again as usual the next time you open them.
* **🖱️ Effortless One-Click Workflow:** Comes with a convenient Desktop shortcut. Just double-click, press `Enter` (Return) in PowerShell, and you're done.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Download** the files from this repository (or the latest Release).
2. **Place the shortcut** (button) onto your **Desktop**.
3. Whenever you finish working, **double-click** the DevCleanup icon on your desktop.
4. A PowerShell window will open—simply press **`Enter` (Return)** to start the cleanup.
5. **Enjoy!** Your PC is immediately cleaner, lighter, and much faster.

---

## 💻 System Requirements

* **Operating System:** Windows 10 / Windows 11
* **Dependencies:** None! Uses built-in Windows PowerShell.
